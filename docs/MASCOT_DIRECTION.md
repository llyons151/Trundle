# Mascot direction

Decided September 23, 2026. Replaces the faceted grey rock as Trundle's mascot.
The art brief and final design are still to come. The app was wiped for a
from-scratch rebuild on September 24, 2026, so no rock art remains in use.

## Decision: a raccoon

Trundle is a raccoon. Raccoons are night creatures, and he's trying to fix his
sleep schedule alongside you: you're both becoming morning people.

Why a raccoon:

- His natural black eye mask already looks like dark circles, so he looks tired
  with no extra drawing. A real sleep mask on top is the visual joke.
- The nocturnal backstory gives him a reason to care about bedtime that people relate to.
- The internet already sees raccoons as sassy ("trash panda"), which suits his personality.
- He has a clear silhouette (round body, pointy ears, striped tail), and he can wrap
  his tail around himself like a blanket.

He should feel like a real creature, simplified into a bold shape the way
Duolingo's owl is. He is not a blob in a costume.

## Personality

Permanently tired, groggy, and a bit of a diva about it. He's sassy about the
*situation* (being awake, mornings, the glow of your phone at 1am) but never shames
the user. He sounds like a best friend who's done with everyone, not a disappointed
parent. This keeps the GAME_PLAN rule: no guilt or punishment.

The core idea: your phone is what's keeping him up. Blocking apps at bedtime is
you finally letting him sleep, and the morning walk is dragging him out of bed.

## Voice samples

| Moment | Line |
|---|---|
| Bedtime | "Phone down. I'm not asking." |
| Opening a blocked app | "Shh. I'm sleeping. So are they." |
| Opening Trundle at 2am | "Why are we awake." |
| Morning, 0 steps | "No." |
| 80 / 200 | "I can hear you walking. I'm ignoring it." |
| 160 / 200 | "Fine. *Fine.*" |
| 200 / 200 | "I'm up. Don't talk to me yet." |
| Nap | "Tucked in. Do not perceive me." |
| Emergency bypass | "I'll allow it. This once. Maybe." |
| Daytime | "I'm awake. Technically." |

The screen shown when a blocked app is opened is where people will screenshot
him most, so his lines there need to be his funniest.

Key video moment: at 200 steps the sleep mask comes up and he gives you a look of
pure betrayal because the sun is out.

## Props (a signature look, not a costume)

- A sleep mask: pulled down at night, pushed up on his forehead during the day.
- A blanket, or his own tail wrapped around him, to show asleep, waking, and awake
  at a glance.

## Video ideas

- "POV: my sleep app has more attitude than me"
- A morning hostage negotiation: film his lines as the step count goes up
- "He goes to bed at 10:30 and apparently so do I now"
- Screenshots of the blocked-app screen ("shh.") as a meme format
- Stitches of people's funniest thing he said to them

## Runner-up: Pallas's cat (manul)

The user flagged this one to keep: it's funny looking. Pallas's cat is the backup
mascot, or a second character (for example a roommate or rival to the raccoon).

- It has the grumpiest face in nature: a flat, unimpressed stare that's pure sass
  with no acting required.
- It's round, fluffy, and low to the ground, with short legs, small ears set
  low on the sides of the head, and a thick striped tail. It would make a very
  good plushie.
- Its stubby, chunky shape is simple enough for an app icon or silhouette.
- Most people have never seen one, so "what IS that" comments would help videos
  spread, and nobody else owns the look.
- It's a real animal with plenty of viral zoo clips that people already find funny.
- Downside: people need a second to recognize what it is, and it doesn't have the
  raccoon's built-in dark-circles joke or nocturnal backstory.

The same personality works: permanently tired, sassy about mornings, and never
shaming the user. The manul's resting face would be the "don't talk to me yet" shot.

## Other candidates (kept for reference)

1. **Koala:** the sleepiest real animal (up to 22 hours a day), with a naturally grumpy
   face. Downside: Pokémon's Komala is already a koala that's always asleep.
2. **Possum:** already looks frazzled, "playing dead" works as falling asleep, and it's
   an internet icon. Downside: some people find possums gross, not cute.
3. **Pug:** the snoring, the squashed grumpy face and the drama all fit. Downside: dogs
   are a crowded space, and a real breed is hard to make your own.
4. **Red panda:** very cute, with tear-mark stripes that could read as tired. Downside:
   it's more sweet than sassy.

Earlier brainstormed concepts, with the species left open:

- **Blanket burrito:** wrapped in a blanket with only eyes and a tuft showing; the
  blanket slides off as you walk and becomes a cape during the day.
- **Sleep mask on the forehead:** big droopy eyes and eye bags; the mask snaps down at bedtime.
- **Droopy nightcap:** a floppy cap that shows his mood and perks up in the morning.
- **Hoodie gremlin:** a small round creature in an oversized hoodie with the
  strings pulled tight, like a burnt-out roommate.

## Ruled out

- **Trolls or anything troll-like:** Trundle is a troll champion in League of Legends
  (Riot Games). Avoid tusks, a club, a hunched giant, or an icy blue look.
- **Look-alikes of existing characters:** Duolingo's owl, Finch's bird, Snorlax,
  Gudetama, and Totoro or other Ghibli spirits.
- **Mascots chosen only because they fit an app feature** (for example, a mole
  that hears your footsteps): clever on paper, but the character and personality
  are what make a mascot catch on.
